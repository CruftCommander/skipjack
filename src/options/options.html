Detection

For videos SponsorBlock users haven't fully marked, Skipjack can read the video's captions and ask an AI model where the pitches are. It uses your own Anthropic API key. Results are kept on this computer for 60 days, so a video you watch again isn't paid for again; a 20-minute video costs about 2 cents on Claude Sonnet.

The key is stored only in this browser on this computer, and is sent only to Anthropic.

When to run detection

Saved results list the videos Skipjack analyzed. Clearing them removes every saved result and every video held back after a failure; videos are analyzed again, and paid for again, the next time you watch them.

Channels

Skipjack skips nothing on these channels and doesn't look their videos up. You can also switch a channel from the Skipjack toolbar button while its video is open.

    No excluded channels.
